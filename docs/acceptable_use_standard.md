# Acceptable Use Standard

Status: template requiring Information Security, Privacy, Legal, and business approval.

Use only approved platform tenants, capabilities, sources, and publishing scopes. A catalog entry is not authorization. Respect source permissions, copyright, confidentiality, and organizational data classification. Verify outputs before operational use. Do not treat model output as an authoritative legal, clinical, financial, or personnel decision.

Do not enter confidential, claimant, employee, or production data unless the exact use case, contract, platform posture, source permissions, retention position, and appropriate data owner authorization explicitly permit it. Do not enter credentials or secrets. Never use a personal connection to circumvent shared-connection review. Do not enable external write actions without explicit risk review.

## Suspected Violation Procedure

1. Log the suspected incident without copying the sensitive prompt or source data. Use references to the approved incident-management system.
2. Record platform, tenant, owner, occurrence time, category, severity, description, and status.
3. Escalate high/critical incidents immediately to Information Security and record both recipient and escalation reference. The API rejects high/critical records without these fields.
4. For an escalated incident of any severity, record the recipient and reference. Escalation is recorded only: this application does not send email, page staff, or open external tickets.
5. Preserve relevant vendor event IDs and configuration evidence through the approved security process. Restrict access and follow legal-hold instructions.
6. Record containment, assessment, and corrective actions. Resolution requires escalation details and a resolution note.

Template severity: low for contained policy uncertainty without sensitive data; medium for likely restricted use with limited exposure; high for suspected confidential/personal/production data exposure; critical for active widespread exposure, compromise, or urgent legal risk. Information Security owns final classification and notification obligations.
